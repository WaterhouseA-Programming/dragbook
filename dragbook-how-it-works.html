Simple Process

How DragBook Works

Whether you're booking a performer for your event or listing your act for the world to find — it takes minutes.

01
🔍
Search for performers

Tell us what you need — a hen party host, wedding crasher, drag bingo queen, corporate MC. Filter by location, style, budget and availability. Every performer on DragBook is verified and reviewed by real event organisers.

📍 Filter by location 🎭 Filter by act type 💷 Set your budget 📅 Check availability
02
💬
Send an enquiry

Found someone you like? Send them a direct enquiry with your event details — date, venue, guest count, and what you're after. No middlemen. No agency calls. The performer gets your message directly and typically responds within a few hours.

⚡ Most respond in 2 hours 📩 Direct messages only 🚫 No agency fees
03
🔒
Confirm and pay a deposit

Once you've agreed the details, confirm your booking through DragBook. A deposit is held securely on the platform — it only releases to the performer after your event. You're protected against no-shows and last-minute cancellations.

🛡️ Deposit protected 💳 Secure payment ✅ Cancellation cover
04
🎉
Enjoy your event — then review

Your performer arrives, does their thing, and absolutely makes the night. After the event, leave a review to help other bookers and release the deposit. That's it — pure fabulousness, start to finish.

⭐ Leave a review 💰 Deposit auto-released 🔁 Easy to rebook
01
✍️
Create your free profile

Sign up and build your performer profile in minutes. Add your act name, photos, show reel, event specialisms, location, and your pricing. The Free tier is genuinely free — no credit card, no tricks.

🆓 Free forever tier 📸 Upload photos & video 💷 Set your own rates 📍 Set your travel range
02
✅
Get verified

Verified performers get significantly more bookings. Submit a short clip of a live performance, confirm your identity and public liability insurance, and we'll review within 48 hours. Verification is free.

🎬 Submit a clip 🪪 ID verification 48hr turnaround ✨ Verified badge unlocked
03
📩
Receive and respond to enquiries

When a booker finds you and sends an enquiry, you'll get notified immediately. Review the event details, ask any questions, and confirm when you're happy. You're in control — accept only what works for you.

🔔 Instant notifications 💬 Direct messaging 📅 Calendar management ❌ Decline anything
04
💰
Perform and get paid

Do what you do best. After the event, the booker releases your deposit and DragBook transfers your payment — minus just 3%. No agency taking 20–35%. No waiting weeks. Your money, in your account.

Only 3% platform fee ⚡ Fast payout 🏦 Direct bank transfer 📊 Earnings dashboard

Questions

Got Questions?

Completely free. You pay the performer's stated fee — nothing is added on top by DragBook. The 3% platform fee comes out of the performer's payment, not yours.

Your deposit is fully refunded if a performer cancels. We also help you find a replacement quickly. Performers who cancel are flagged on the platform and may lose their verified status.

Verified performers have submitted a live performance clip, undergone identity verification, and confirmed they hold public liability insurance. The Verified badge only appears on profiles that have passed all three checks.

DragBook is currently UK-focused, but many performers are available for international events at their discretion. Check individual profiles for travel availability and use the enquiry form to discuss international bookings directly.

Most verified performers respond within 2–4 hours. Each profile shows the performer's typical response time. If you need someone urgently, filter by "Available this weekend" and message several performers at once.
